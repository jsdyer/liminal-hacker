## ferry

Rows of faded ocean-blue seats, muted orange safety lamps, an abandoned-looking but cared-for interior. On right, a small wooden table with open Linux laptop and a warm mug. Vast quiet space.

## planetarium

The analogue star projector silhouettes in middle distance. On right a tiny control desk with open laptop and amber task light. Cosmic silence, old civic architecture.

## bowling

## parkade

An enormous empty underground concrete parking level at 04:08. Repeating pillars and gentle puddle reflections, pale green fluorescent strips, a small orange exit glow far away. At right a maintenance alcove holds a humble folding desk, open laptop, thermos and warm work light. Deep long perspective and cozy refuge inside vast architecture.

## relay

Inside a remote mountain radio relay station at 02:38. Broad windows show dark conifers, fog and faint red antenna lights. Spacious concrete control hall, quietly sleeping vintage radio racks, cables carefully organized, a lone open laptop on workbench at right under a warm brass light. Cold slate blue outside, warm amber within.

## campus

A single desk at right with glowing open laptop, red-orange desk lamp and a tea mug. Cool muted cyan fluorescent light, generous depth, no readable screen text.

## skybridge

At right near the window a tiny reading nook with chair, open laptop and amber lamp. Architecture impossibly still, subtle uneasy symmetry.

## archive

At right an old microfilm desk with open laptop and small amber banker lamp beside a stack of archival boxes. Dusty olive and warm bronze palette, profoundly empty.
